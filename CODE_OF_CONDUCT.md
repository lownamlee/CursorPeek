# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

CursorPeek contributors, maintainers, and users should make participation welcoming, respectful,
and constructive. Examples of expected behavior include:

- showing empathy and patience;
- giving specific, technical feedback without attacking people;
- respecting different experience levels, identities, and viewpoints;
- accepting responsibility, apologizing, and learning from mistakes;
- prioritizing the health of the project and its community.

Harassment, threats, sexualized attention, discriminatory language, deliberate intimidation,
doxxing, sustained disruption, and publishing another person's private information are not
acceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone is publicly representing CursorPeek. It
covers issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, qualification work, and other project
communication.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to a repository maintainer. Use a private contact method
published on the maintainer's GitHub profile. If no private contact is available, open a public
issue requesting private contact but include no sensitive details.

Maintainers will protect the reporter's privacy as far as reasonably possible, review conflicts of
interest, and respond in proportion to the behavior. Responses may include a private correction,
warning, temporary restriction, removal from project spaces, or permanent exclusion. A maintainer
named in a report must not participate in deciding its outcome.

Good-faith reports will not result in retaliation. Knowingly false or malicious reports may
themselves violate this policy.
